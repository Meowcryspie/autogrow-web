## 2. COMPONENT ARCHITECTURE BLUEPRINT

### A. The Hero Section
* Split-screen layout:
  * **Left (55%):** Left-aligned headline, New Zealand engineering decal, factual summary, primary CTA button (`Request Commercial Quote`) + secondary (`Explore MGC-Pro Specs`).
  * **Right (45%):** Clean daylight **MultiGrow Master Console** showing real-time 4-compartment telemetry, active solenoid state, and CAD terminal wiring block.
  * **Bottom:** 4-column daylight spec banner (`32 INDEPENDENT COMPARTMENTS`, `100 m³/hr DOSING CAPACITY`, `±0.03 EC DOSING TOLERANCE`, `IP66 / NEMA 4X DIE-CAST ENCLOSURE`).

### B. Plant-Centric Crop Solutions Section
* Interactive biological steering selector:
  * High-Wire Vine Crops (Tomatoes, Capsicums)
  * Tabletop Berries & Soft Fruit (Strawberries, Blueberries)
  * Leafy Greens NFT Channels (Lettuce, Herbs)
  * Indoor Vertical CEA (Microgreens, Seedlings)
* Real SVG **24-Hour Volumetric Water Content (VWC%) dryback curve** demonstrating generative vs. vegetative irrigation steering.

### C. Hardware & Skid Product Grid
* Crisp white chassis panels (`.panel-chassis`) with subtle borders and subtle daylight shadows.
* Silkscreen model tags (`[MGC-Pro]`, `[SGF-100]`, `[IDC-4]`, `[ICC-2]`, `[EPS-Pro]`, `[WCS-Pro]`).
* Direct clickable **Technical Pinouts & Schematics** modal with full terminal blocks (`TB1 RS-485`, `TB2 4-20mA`, `TB3 24VAC`).

### D. The Phone Scaling Studio (`IntelliGrow™ Mobile`)
* Replicates TrolMaster’s 1:1 mobile scaling showcase:
  * Viewport presets: Compact (340px), Standard (380px), Pro Max (420px).
  * Zoom magnification slider ($75\% - 115\%$) + instant reset.
  * Interactive app tabs: Sensors, Dosing, Substrate, Alarms.
  * **Live pulse dosing trigger** that updates live EC/pH values in real time.

### E. Commercial ROI & Payback Calculator
* Real input sliders for Growing Area (Hectares), Annual Fertilizer Spend ($), and Annual Water Spend ($).
* Instant mathematical breakdown of fertilizer recaptured (~22%), water conserved (~26%), and cumulative 5-year commercial ROI with 4.8-month breakeven trajectory.

### F. Global Distributor Locator & Technical Downloads
* Regional locator across Australasia (Global HQ), North America, Europe, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific.
* Filterable technical downloads library for production firmware (v1.4.2), CAD 3D DXF files, wiring guides, and CE/FCC compliance certificates.
