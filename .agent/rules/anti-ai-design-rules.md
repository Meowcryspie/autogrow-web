# ANTI-AI INDUSTRIAL DESIGN DIRECTIVE (AVOID vs. DO)

Every front-end component, layout, CSS style, and piece of copy must strictly follow this AVOID vs. DO specification:

## 1. Color & Lighting
* ❌ **AVOID:** Glowing neon borders (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px #00ff88`), blurry radial gradient background orbs, and purple/indigo gradients (`bg-indigo-600`).
* ✅ **DO:** Use matte carbon surfaces (`#070B08`, `#0E1410`, `#161F19`), crisp 1px hairline dividers (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), top surface chamfer bevels, and reserve signal green (`#00E575`) *strictly* for active system states (10% rule).

## 2. Layout & Composition
* ❌ **AVOID:** Symmetrical 3-column card rows with centered headlines and uniform 20px padding.
* ✅ **DO:** Use asymmetric split consoles (60/40 or 70/30), left-aligned anchor typography, edge-bleeding split panels, and high-density spec matrices alternating with generous photographic breathing room (96px–128px vertical padding).

## 3. Typography & Data
* ❌ **AVOID:** Default fonts (Inter, Roboto, Arial), uniform text scale, and jittering numerical displays.
* ✅ **DO:** 
  - Headline/Models: Bold uppercase industrial grotesque (`Rajdhani`, 700/800, `letter-spacing: +0.04em`).
  - Body: Humanist/geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 400 Regular, `line-height: 1.65`).
  - Telemetry: Monospace (`IBM Plex Mono`) with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for rock-steady data readouts.

## 4. Media & Hardware Tactility
* ❌ **AVOID:** Generic 48×48px icons trapped in colorful rounded boxes, cheesy 3D clay graphics, or stock marketing avatars.
* ✅ **DO:** Present physical industrial equipment: laser-etched silkscreen decals (`.decal-badge`), real terminal block legends (`TB1 RS-485`, `TB2 Analog`, `TB3 Relays`), DIN-rail mounting specs, and IP66 die-cast ratings.

## 5. Interactions & Motion
* ❌ **AVOID:** Floaty spring/bounce physics (`cubic-bezier(0.68, -0.55, 0.27, 1.55)`), hover zooms that inflate cards wildly.
* ✅ **DO:** Mechanical relay snappiness: `120ms–160ms` duration, crisp deceleration (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), physical button depression (`translateY(1px)`), and instant hairline border highlights.

## 6. Copywriting & Tone
* ❌ **AVOID:** Vague marketing fluff ("seamless", "elevate", "unleash the power", "smart solution").
* ✅ **DO:** Factual agricultural engineering specifications: cite tolerances (±0.03 EC, ±0.05 pH), flow rates (up to 100 m³/hr), isolation (2.5 kV RMS), and plant-centric metrics (VPD in kPa, VWC%, daily dryback %).
